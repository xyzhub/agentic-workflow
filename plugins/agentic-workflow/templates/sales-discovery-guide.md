Earn the right to pitch by diagnosing first. This guide qualifies a buyer *before*

## Qualification (budget · authority · need · timing)

| Dimension | What you're testing | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | Is there money, or would this be net-new spend nobody owns? | _"How do initiatives like this usually get funded on your side?"_ |
| **Authority** | Who signs, who champions, who can veto? | _"Besides you, who'd weigh in on a decision like this?"_ |
| **Need** | Is the pain real and urgent, or a nice-to-have? | _"How are you handling (the problem) today, and where does it hurt?"_ |
| **Timing** | Is there a trigger making now the moment? | _"What's changed recently that put this on your list?"_ |

## Open discovery questions → mapped to a pillar

Ask these open; listen for the buyer's own language and reuse it. Each maps to a
messaging **pillar** from `positioning.md` — the answer tells you whether that
pillar will land.

1. _What does (the problem area) cost you in a normal week?_ → **Pillar: (problem/cost)**
2. _What have you tried, and where did it fall short?_ → **Pillar: (differentiator)**
3. _Walk me through the last time (the problem) bit you — what happened?_ → **Pillar: (problem/cost)**
4. _Who else feels this, and who signs off on fixing it?_ → **Pillar: (buyer/urgency)**
5. _What changed recently that makes this worth solving now?_ → **Pillar: (why-now)**
6. _If this were solved, what would change for you specifically?_ → **Pillar: (key benefit)**
7. _What would "good" look like six months in?_ → **Pillar: (key benefit)**
8. _What's made you hesitate on tools like this before?_ → **Pillar: (differentiator)**
9. _Where would this have to fit in your existing workflow?_ → **Pillar: (category fit)**
10. _If we did nothing, what happens?_ → **Pillar: (cost of inaction)**

_Replace the parenthetical pillar names with this venture's actual pillars from
`positioning.md` — the mapping is the point: a question whose pillar you can't
name isn't earning its place._

## Disqualify-fast signals

Walk away early when these show — chasing a bad-fit deal costs both sides:

- **No trigger, no urgency** — the pain is tolerable and nothing changed; there's
  no "why now". (Don't manufacture one.)
- **No budget path** — the spend would be net-new and nobody owns it.
- **No authority in the room** — you can't find who signs, and the champion can't
  get you to them.
- **Need is a nice-to-have** — the buyer can't name what solving it changes.
- **Outside the ICP** — the account doesn't match the fit criteria above; the
  product isn't built for their case.
